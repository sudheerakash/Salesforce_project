==========================================
Student Validation Rule
==========================================

Validation Rule Name:
Validate_Student_Year

Object:
Student__c

Description:
Ensures that the student year is between 1 and 4.

Error Condition Formula:

OR(
    Year__c < 1,
    Year__c > 4
)

Error Message:
Year must be between 1 and 4.

Error Location:
Field: Year
