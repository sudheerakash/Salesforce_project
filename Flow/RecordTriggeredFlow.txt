==========================================
Record-Triggered Flow
==========================================

Flow Name:
Student Record Triggered Flow

Flow Type:
Record-Triggered Flow

Object:
Student__c

Trigger:
A record is created.

Run:
Before the record is saved.

Purpose:
Automatically assign a default phone number if the Phone field is left blank while creating a new Student record.

------------------------------------------
Flow Logic
------------------------------------------

Start
    |
    V
Student Record Created
    |
    V
Decision:
Is Phone__c blank?
    |
   Yes
    |
    V
Assignment:
Phone__c = '9999999999'
    |
    V
Save Record
    |
    V
End

------------------------------------------
Business Scenario
------------------------------------------

When a new student record is created without entering a phone number,
the flow automatically assigns the default value:

9999999999

This ensures that every student record contains a phone number.

Test Cases

------------------------------------------
End of Flow
